I recently started playing **Cyberpunk 2077**. I had once previously played it during COVID lockdown when I had just gotten my first (*also still my current*) Gaming Laptop (*HP Omen 15 en0002ax*). But I was rather young to understand the story, plus it was buggy and didn't make me want to continue playing, I uninstalled it after playing first few quests.

---

Also, last month I watched **Cyberpunk: Edgerunners**, which is when I decided to give Cyberpunk 2077 another chance. And I got really into playing it. The game amazed me a lot. I'm getting into the story a lot.

I see Cyberpunk as a **very strong IP** to have, which dragged me into the history of **CD PROJEKT RED**. It really amazed me how a small video game importer and distributor company founded by two high school friends grew into a company with net worth around **$6.27 billion USD** (at the time I'm writing this blog).

The history of the studio itself is a really inspiring story. There are rumours that **Project Orion** (Cyberpunk 2) is under development, which I'm excited for. I have heard that the studio has moved to **Unreal Engine** for their future projects. At first I was confused why, then it made sense because it'd make the hiring process easy because they won't have to **maintain** a massive in-house game engine and also train every new employee on how to use it. I'm **indecisive** on whether I'm in favor of the industry moving to established software like Unreal Engine or not, as other studios including **Halo Studios** (formerly 343 Industries), **Crystal Dnyamics** (makers of Tomb Raider), and more have also moved to Unreal Engine for their projects. Doing so they lose some amount of **fine-grain control** over their projects and are mostly dependent on Epic Games for new features (altough they can add new features themselves thanks to Unreal Engine being open-source).

The expansion of the game into a **dedicated anime series** was something I'd have never imagined would be done. Even if done, I wouldn't have expected it to be soooo good. It is no less than a **masterpiece** itself. The soundtrack is amazing and remarkable as well.

I'm *actively waiting* for season 2 of Cyberpunk: Edgerunners.
