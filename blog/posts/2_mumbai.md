## Background

Until this point, I had been living in my hometown **Solapur** (Small tier-3 city in the state of Maharashtra, India) all my life. From birth to schooling to my Diploma, all in this city alone.

As now I have completed my Diploma in Computer Engineering from VVP Polytechnic under MSBTE (Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education) with an aggregate percentage of 97.12. I was able to secure my seat at Sardar Patel Institute of Technology, Mumbai, which is amongst top 3 colleges for engineering in the state of Maharashtra. I secured the seat for the Computer Engineering program as a Direct Second Year (Lateral Entry) student.

## College excitement

I was in Pune when I found out that I had got the college I wanted. I was quite excited and happy. Then finally came the time where I left my hometown for the dream city of Mumbai. I got my admission on 16th of August, 2026 and immediately started attending college from the very next day. As Direct Entry Students, we join late after the regular classes have already started for around a month. Hence, I still do not understand much that's being taught until I cover what I missed. Every direct second year entry student goes through this same problem in the entire state.

## Searching for PG to stay

Me and my Dad had booked an AirBNB for two days near Versova beach in order to search for PGs for me to live in. We searched for two days continously, almost all the places we looked were very ill-managed and unhygenic, for example 6 people in an 1 BHK with only one toilet. Seeing those I was quite disgusted, but the next day a miracle happened, we contacted a broker who showed us this amazing 2 BHK flat shared by four people. It was clean, tidy, it so happened that one of the four was leaving due to his personal issues, which cleared a spot for me.

I felt quite relieved when we finally found a good place to stay. Another advantage was even my Dad could stay there temporarily without any extra fees. The flatmates are very humble and some of the kindest people.

The flat took care of a lot of my problems including food, cook didi comes and cooks breakfast, lunch, and dinner. There's a washing machine for clothes. Room is equipped with AC. There's a study table already in place for me to use.

Finding a place like this in a place like Andheri that too near a metro station is like finding a needle in a haystack. I'm glad I found this place.

## The Direct Second Year Shock

When I joined, few days later Mid-Sem exams are announced just 15 days later. I was shocked, how could one cover the syllabus for all subjects in just 15 days when I hadn't even gotten used to living there. I looked through PYQs, those PYQs worsened my anxiety because they were nothing like I was used to. All the questions were analytical and application-based. Plus, there was mathematics which I hadn't touched in 2 whole years, because Diploma only had Math in the first year. I started to think about dropping out, cancelling admission, and moving back to my hometown. I attended a week of college, then I was seriously burnt out. Fortunately, the exam department decided to give us direct second year students the option to either cancel the MidSem exams or give it. I chose to cancel all MidSem exams so I get time to actually study in peace.

Fortunately, I made two good friends on the first day. One I had found online before college started and another on the first day. We three make a good trio and stay together in college. But they both are Mumbai locals.

## Battling Loneliness after College

College functions from 9 am to 5 pm on Monday and Tuesday and till 4 pm from Wednesday till Friday. When I come back to the apartment, I'm mostly alone because everyone else is at work and they return around 7-8pm. So I feel quite lonely. To battle that I have decided to spend more time in college library after lectures, that I'll inact after going back.

## MidSem Holidays

So, after the first week, I attended college for 2 days in second week. There was holiday for Eid on Wednesday and Rakshabandhan on Friday, the faculty decided to keep online classes on Thursday to make it easier for out-of-station students to visit home to celebrate. Hence, I came back after lunch on Tuesday and took my train back to Solapur. And the week after (this week) were MidSem exams which I chose to cancel so it is basically holidays for me, hence I'm writing this blog post.

## What next?

I'll be heading back to Mumbai coming Monday (7th September) and continue my journey at SPIT. I hope to keep blog readers like you updated if anything interesting or worth sharing happens.
