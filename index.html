bio
I'm a Computer Engineering student at SPIT, Mumbai. I've always been someone who likes understanding software at every layer, from building functional apps and websites to working close to the metal with OpenGL and DirectX. That low-level curiosity is what first pulled me into serious software development, and it's stuck with me ever since. When the AI wave hit, it pulled me in too, and that's where most of my energy goes now, building RAG pipelines and upskilling on state-of-the-art ML techniques. I like that my foundation is broad; it means when I'm working on AI systems, I'm not just thinking about models in isolation, but about how they fit into real, working software. I recently started writing about what I'm building and thinking on my blog.
